# TintinTV Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user watches films and series at home, including on mobile and a television-sized display. The site also has an administrator configuration workflow when backed by a supported server-side storage provider.

## Product Purpose

TintinTV helps users browse film and television metadata, search third-party video sources, choose an episode or source, and resume watching. It does not host the video files.

## Operating Context

The home page highlights a current title and content rows. Users can browse films, series, and variety shows; search; keep favorites and viewing history; and switch playback sources when one fails. Playback needs to work well from a distance and on a narrow touch screen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing search, favorites, history, source selection, authentication, and admin behavior.
- Featured artwork and browse content are provided dynamically; a title may have only a portrait poster, not a widescreen backdrop.
- Third-party video or image sources may be slow or unavailable. Loading and error states must remain usable.
- Admin configuration is unavailable with local storage; server-backed environments may support it.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: TintinTV. Use its existing logo rather than Apple marks or copied Apple artwork.
- The Apple TV reference is a direction for cinematic hierarchy and focused navigation, not a claim of affiliation.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a logo, existing film metadata and poster integrations, and functional pages for home, browse, search, playback, login, and admin.

## Product Principles

- Let the selected film or series lead; controls support the content.
- Make search, source switching, and recovery quick when a source fails.
- Keep focus, contrast, and target sizes clear on both remote-like keyboard and touch input.
